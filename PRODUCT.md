## Platform

## Stack

Interactive islands only where needed: quote wizard (no prices), before/after slider, airflow/cleaning visualization (GSAP, Three.js only if it earns its weight). ## Users
Both audiences equally (confirmed):
- Private homeowners and apartment owners with mini-central (ducted) or wall-mounted units. Decide fast, price-sensitive, need trust before letting a crew into the home.
- Businesses and institutions (offices, VRF systems). Decide slower, need an orderly quote, documentation and scheduling around operations.
Home page serves both; a separate business path leads to "פתרונות לעסקים ומוסדות".

## Product Purpose
Lead generation for "המרכז למיזוג וחיטוי": turn visitors into quote requests via WhatsApp, phone, short form, or the quote wizard. Success = qualified inquiries at a cost that fits the ₪10–25 per-inquiry target the ads are run against.

## Positioning
Deep duct cleaning, not just filter washing: the core message is that cleaning filters does not clean the ducts. Mechanism (as stated by the owner, to be shown with real footage): American duct-cleaning method, 3,600W extraction unit, HEPA filtration, dedicated duct brushes, before/after documentation.

## Operating Context
Service areas: Kiryat Gat to Hadera and surroundings, Jerusalem area (matches ad targeting). Owner says "nationwide"; site copy must not contradict the actual service area until confirmed. Leads arrive via WhatsApp and phone; Meta and TikTok ads drive traffic to the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Services: duct cleaning and disinfection; mini-central units; wall-mounted units; VRF systems; offices/businesses/institutions; mold, dust and dirt buildup.
THE SITE IS THE BUSINESS'S STOREFRONT (owner decision, 25.11.2026): no prices, no promotions, no offers, no discounts, no seasonal deals anywhere (no price list, no amounts, no "50% off", no winter offer section). Seasonal topics (e.g. preparing the system for heating) appear only as service/problem-solving content. Quote wizard collects system type, number of ducts, property type, city and add-ons, then sends the details to WhatsApp or as a callback request; the price is given in conversation.
Content focus (owner): advantages, FAQ/objections, problem-solving (odor, dust, mold, winter heating prep), testimonials.
Guarantee: one-year warranty per warranty terms (terms text: OPEN). "No result, no payment" per service terms (terms text: OPEN).
Open: phone number, WhatsApp number, business registration details, exact warranty/offer terms, the "2,000+ customers" figure (owner must verify before use).

## Product Principles
1. Show, don't claim: every advantage is backed by real footage, a real photo, or written terms.
2. A storefront, not a flyer: no prices or promotions; explain what the job includes and what affects the quote, and answer objections, but never show amounts.
3. Two doors, one site: homeowners get speed and reassurance; businesses get process and documentation.
4. No unsupported health claims: talk about dust, odor, mold buildup and maintenance, not medical outcomes (EPA states duct cleaning has not been shown to prevent health problems).
5. One tap to talk: WhatsApp and call are always reachable on mobile.
